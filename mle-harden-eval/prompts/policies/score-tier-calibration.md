# Internal score-tier calibration policy

Freeze baseline, bronze, silver, and gold only after all five GPT-5.5 slots and every required reference-improvement decision are terminal. Use the cold benchmark ladder, the five direction-aware GPT outcomes, and the current reproducible reference together. Each boundary must be finite, correctly ordered, separated by a meaningful capability change rather than display precision, and stable relative to ordinary rerun variation.

The competitive baseline must remain above simple heuristics and generic one-shot work. Bronze begins medal-competitive performance, silver represents a credible strong solution, and gold remains beatable by the current reference with a reproducibility margin. Record every scored and failed GPT slot, the reference score, prior ladder evidence, direction, and rationale. Once approved, the tier revision is immutable and cannot be adjusted after Qwen results are observed.
